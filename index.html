CardiacGPT

A voice-driven AI co-pilot for the cardiac operating room

Three role-specific agents, one each for the anesthesiologist, surgeon, and perfusionist, answer clinical questions during surgery. Each agent retrieves from more than 1,000 drug and clinical literature embeddings and returns weight-based dosing guidance in under three seconds.

I built the agent orchestration in LangGraph, the retrieval layer in ChromaDB, and the production backend on FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and WebSockets, including a HIPAA-grade de-identification layer that keeps patient identifiers away from the model.

LangGraphClaude APIChromaDBFastAPIPostgreSQLWebSockets

How it stays trustworthy

  • Every answer cites its sourcesClinicians can see which documents a recommendation came from.
  • Failures retry, then fail safeIf evidence still can't be found, it says so instead of guessing a dose.
  • Clinicians reviewed the outputsThe cardiac surgery team checked answers for accuracy and usefulness.
  • Patient data never reaches the modelIdentifiers are stripped before any request is sent.
